Game features

Small game. Sharp claws.

Every system is built around one idea: make the next jump feel good. Movement, combat and procedural platforms all feed into the climb.

01

Jump on your terms

Land, breathe, then choose the moment. Manual jumping with coyote time and a small jump buffer keeps the controls forgiving.

02

Rocket boost

Fire a high jump on a 4.2-second cooldown. Recover from a miss, skip a gap, or chase a platform that looks just out of reach.

03

A longer blade

A wider slash arc and more reach. Cut through swamp mobs with X, J, a click, or the on-screen sword.

04

Stomp the mobs

Drop on a mob from above to squash it and bounce. Or take the cleaner route and slash it to pieces.

05

Three hearts

A hit costs a heart and grants a moment of invulnerability. Lose all three and the swamp wins this round.

06

Never the same climb

Platforms, mobs and springs are generated as you go. Every run has a new layout; your best height is saved locally.

FAQ

Good questions, quick answers.

Everything you need to know before your first climb.

Is Croc Jump free?

Yes. It is completely free to play, with no account, ads or download.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a modern browser on desktop or phone. A recent version of Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox is recommended.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Use the on-screen arrows, the gold jump button, the rocket button and the sword button. Portrait orientation is recommended.

How is my best score saved?

Your best height is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data or using a different browser resets it.

Is there an end?

No. Platforms are generated endlessly. The goal is simple: climb higher than your last run.
